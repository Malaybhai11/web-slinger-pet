WEBSLINGER

He doesn’t float over your page — he lives on it. Every button, heading and card is a rooftop. Click any element and he’ll web it and swing. Move him with A/D, jump with Space.

why dom-native?

Most page mascots are stickers — absolutely-positioned GIFs that ignore the page beneath them. This one reads the DOM like a map: headings are ledges, buttons are trampolines, navbars are highways. Layout shifts, lazy-loaded sections, responsive breakpoints — the playground rebuilds itself and he adapts in real time.

the golden rule

Never stand on nothing. Every physics step asks the same question: what real element is under my feet? If the answer is none, gravity wins. It sounds simple, but it’s what makes him feel like he belongs to the page instead of being pasted on top of it.

controls

A/D or arrows to walk, Shift to run, Space/W to jump, S to crouch, and click any element to web-swing from it — click again to let go. On touch screens: left/right thirds to move, tap to shoot.
